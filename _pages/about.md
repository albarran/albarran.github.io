I am Associate Professor (tenured) in the [Department of Economics (FAE)](http://dfae.ua.es/) at the [Universidad de Alicante](https://www.ua.es).

My research focuses on **microeconometrics**, **labor economics**, and **empirical economics**, with applications to inequality, education and health, scientometrics, and household behavior.
